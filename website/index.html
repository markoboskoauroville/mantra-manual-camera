What it does

A camera's controls, not a phone's

Most camera apps give you either a pretty automatic picture or a wall of sliders. This one gives you both, parameter by parameter, and then sends the picture where a production needs it: to a card, to NDI, to a screen-share, to OBS.

01

Manual where you want it

ISO, shutter, focus and white balance each on a thick mixer fader with its own A / M switch. Pull focus yourself while the camera keeps exposure. Shutter presets in degrees, ISO presets at the sensor's cleanest and highest.

02

Log from the cinema cameras

Sony S-Log3, Panasonic V-Log, ARRI LogC3 and LogC4, Blackmagic Film Gen5, in the phone's own tone mapper: in the file and on the stream,.

03

10-bit HLG HEVC

With no log curve, the phone's own broadcast HLG: a soft highlight shoulder like film, a wide gamut, none of the smartphone processing that flattens light. Half the size of H.264.

04

NDI HX and full NDI

The phone appears as a camera in vMix, OBS, Wirecast, NDI Studio Monitor. Over Wi-Fi, or over a USB cable with no network at all.

05

A truly clean feed

One key, FULL, and there is nothing on the glass but the picture. Mirror the phone to a computer and it is ready to broadcast.

06

Focus you can point at

Tap anywhere to focus there. Pinch the mark from an eye to the whole frame. A focus puller with keyframes on a distance scale racks from one to the next at the speed you choose.

07

Look and exposure tools

Your own .cube LUTs, focus peaking, false colour and zebra, drawn by the GPU on the monitor only, so the recording keeps its full range.

08

White balance like a cinema camera

Kelvin from tungsten to daylight. Tap the triangle on something white: it reads, steps and locks neutral in a few moves.

09

A camera's readouts

Record-run timecode, REC, the free space and the hours left on the drive, to the phone or a USB-C SSD. Every lens one key. The LED as a light on a fader. An UPDATE key brings the newest version.

10

Blender's tracker inside

The focus mark follows a moving subject with libmv, the tracker of Blender's motion-tracking studio, on the phone itself: a swinging ball held in every frame where a plain tracker loses it.

11

Smooth by itself

Hold the focus square or the exposure circle and switch TRACK on: every two seconds it looks again and glides to the new focus, ISO or white balance, and only when the change is real, so a steady shot never pulses.

12

A distance scale you can trust

The focus puller's scale is calibrated against a tape measure: 70 cm reads 70 cm, 3 m reads 3 m. Keyframes land within a hair, every time.

13

Your Mac's webcam, on a cable

On a USB cable the camera's own picture goes straight to a Mac as a webcam for Zoom, Meet and OBS, smooth and without NDI's delay, and the whole camera can be steered there with mouse and keyboard.

14

Two drawers beside REC

LENS and DISPLAY ASSIST sit on either side of the record key. Each opens over the whole rail with large keys (every lens with its millimetres, aperture and resolution; peaking, false colour and zebra) and gives the other keys back when it closes.

15

Time left that tells the truth

The file's bit rate follows the format (720p, 1080p, 4K, 8 or 10-bit, the frame rate), so the time left on the rail changes the moment the format does, shown large beside the battery.

16

Faders that meet the light

Switch a fader to M and a yellow line marks where the meter wants it; slide the cap onto the line and the cap turns yellow. The caps are cut like a mixer's, a line across with a grip either side, and the words on the faders stand upright however the phone is held.

17

The Sony remote, built in

Settings → SONY: the whole Mantra Sony Remote inside the camera. A time code generator, time code into a Sony over USB, a Sony's time code read from its screen by the phone's lens, REC ALL over Bluetooth, the clap, LTC out.

18

Your Sonys on the top line

Switch SONY on and the outputs line says how the Alpha bodies are doing: SONY STBY 0/2 while they wait, ● SONY REC 2/2 while they record, how many of how many.

19

Hold REC: START SONY

Hold the record key two seconds and START SONY appears. One touch starts every Sony over Bluetooth and this camera's take together; hold again for STOP SONY.

20

Time code in every file

Every take carries a free-run time code, the time of day of its first frame, as a time code track that Resolve, Premiere and ffprobe read. It runs from the same generator the Sonys are jammed from, so the phone's clips and the Sonys' line up on the timeline by themselves.

The app

The picture is sacred, the keys live beside it

A phone's screen is wider than 16:9, so the keys go in the black margins and never sit on the shot. Grey is off, green is on, dark is a key this phone cannot honour.

Remote control

Two ways to film a concert

Put a phone on a tripod as a camera and steer it from a second phone or a tablet with Camera Remote, the sister app that comes with every release. The camera's own screen appears on the remote and every touch goes back: record, lens, focus, exposure, white balance, the look. In the room over Wi-Fi, or from anywhere in the world over 4G through a free private network.

01

Phones and Sony cameras together

Phones on tripods as cameras, Sony Alpha bodies beside them, every picture and every control on one tablet.

02

The cameras, steered from a phone

No tablet at all: Camera Remote on one phone runs the camera phones, and Mantra Sony Remote runs the Sony bodies.

Remote focus, lens and exposure

A focus puller on the remote: a distance scale with keyframes, and a rack between them at the speed you set. The phone's lenses are its zoom: wide, main, tele, one key each. Quality per network, set live: frame rate, size and bit rate, Wi-Fi and 4G each remembering their own.

With Sony cameras too

Next to the phones, Sony Alpha bodies join the same tablet through Mantra Sony Remote: one key per camera and a multiview of what is live. The Sony bodies it works with, from the α6000 to the α1, are listed there with where to find each one, new or used.
